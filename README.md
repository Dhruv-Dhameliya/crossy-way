# 🐔 Crossy Way

A fast, colourful hopping game. Guide a little chicken across endless roads, rivers and railway tracks — how far can you get?

## How to play

- **Hop forward** to reach new rows. Every new row scores points.
- **Dodge** cars, trucks and buses. Wait for a gap, then go.
- **Cross rivers** by hopping onto floating logs and lily pads. Don't fall in!
- **Watch the tracks.** Railway signals are **green** when the track is clear and **flash red** (with a bell and a whistle) a few seconds before a train comes.
- **Mind the turtles.** Turtle rivers look friendly, but the turtles shake and then dive — hop off in time!
- **Beware crocodile logs.** You can ride a crocodile's back, but when its mouth starts to open the head is about to snap shut — keep away from the front!
- **Slippery ice.** Hop onto an ice lane and you keep gliding a little (up to 3 tiles sideways, one lane forward or back). Plan your next hop.
- **Chasms.** Some lanes are a bottomless drop with nothing but moving platforms to cross them. Time your hop — miss and you fall.
- **Don't dawdle.** Stand still and you'll hear the **eagle screech**; 3 seconds later it swoops in. Reach a new row to send it away.

You lose if you get hit, fall in the water or a chasm, get eaten by a crocodile, or the eagle gets you.

## Difficulty

Pick **Easy, Medium, Hard or Extreme** in Settings. Harder modes mean faster and busier traffic, more frequent trains, faster rivers and a quicker eagle (Easy 12 s, Medium 9 s, Hard 7.5 s, Extreme 6 s). **Each difficulty keeps its own top score** — you'll see them listed on the difficulty buttons, and the 🏆 chip on screen and the game-over card show the top score for the mode you're playing.

## Scoring

- **Combo** — hop to new rows in quick succession to build a combo. The multiplier climbs from **x1 up to x5**, and every new row is worth the current multiplier. Pause too long and it resets.
- **Close calls** — stand in a lane and let a vehicle sweep past with barely a gap to earn **bonus points** (and keep your combo alive).

## Controls

| Device | Move | Other |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keyboard | Arrow keys or WASD | Space = forward, Esc = pause |
| Touch | Tap = hop forward. Swipe along the lanes you see — diagonal swipes follow the camera angle (Lean Left / Right) | Optional on-screen D-pad in Settings |
| Mouse | Click = hop forward, drag = hop that way | |

**On-screen D-pad (touch screens):** Up/Down sit on one side and Left/Right on the other — you can swap the sides in Settings. While the D-pad is on, taps and swipes on the screen are turned off so nothing happens by accident.

## What's on the road

Cars, taxis, police cars, vans, trucks and buses — plus **motorbikes, tractors, ambulances, ice-cream trucks and sports cars**, each with their own speed and sounds. On the grass, trees are joined by **hay bales, fences, scarecrows, stumps, barrels, sunflowers and mushrooms** that block your way.

## Collect

- 🪙 **Coins** — scattered everywhere; your total is saved.
- **Power-ups** — floating badges that appear now and then:

| | Power-up | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🧲 | Magnet | Pulls nearby coins to you |
| 👻 | Ghost | Traffic passes right through you |
| 🛡️ | Shield | Survive one hit |
| ❄️ | Freeze | Stops all traffic and trains for a few seconds |
| 💰 | 2x Coins | Every coin counts double |
| 💣 | Blast | Blows up the vehicles around you |
| 🦘 | Jump | Hop over small obstacles — stumps, mushrooms, bushes, rocks, hay bales, fences, barrels |
| ⏭️ | Skip Lane | Leap **over a whole lane** — two tiles at once, over anything: traffic, a river, a train track. Risky: you must land safely! |

## Shop 🛍️

Spend your coins on new looks. Pick a **chicken** (Classic, Brownie, Rosy, Sky, Lime, Fire, Midnight, Golden) and dress it up with **outfits** — shades, a mustache, a bow tie, party hat, red cap, cowboy hat, wizard hat or a royal crown. Mix and match: one hat plus one face item at a time.

## Missions 🎯

- **Daily Challenge** — a new goal every day, the same for everyone. Finish it to earn bonus coins and build a 🔥 streak for bigger rewards.
- **Missions** — three rolling goals like "collect 30 coins" or "reach row 50". Finish one and a red dot appears on the Missions button. Open it to watch the bar fill, turn green and slide away, and a fresh mission takes its place.

## Biomes 🚀

The world has more than one look. Run far enough in the **Forest** and a portal carries you into **Space**: the roads become glass walkways with **spaceships** flying over them (fighters, freighters, liners, saucers, asteroid miners, even a flying ice-cream UFO), the rivers become **empty space** that you cross on **broken asteroid chunks** and drifting **satellites** (which power down!), crocodiles become **void worms**, and chasms become **black holes** crossed on wrecked hull plates. Rockets, crystals, monoliths, stranded astronauts, habitat domes, supply crates and little aliens block your way, and your chicken puts on an **astronaut helmet**. It's the same game — only the design and the obstacles change — and the sky fills with drifting dust and meteors (rain becomes a **meteor shower**). In Settings → World you can pick **Auto** (Forest → Space → Forest…), or lock **Forest** or **Space**.

## Share your score 📸

On the game-over card, **Share** makes a picture card — a snapshot of your run, your score, difficulty, biome and top score. Share it straight to your apps, **save** it as an image, or **copy** it.

## The world

The game slowly cycles from **day** to **sunset**, **night** and **dawn** as you run (vehicles switch on their headlights after dark). The **weather** changes too: clear skies, **rain** with thunder and lightning, and **fog**.

## Music & settings

A live-composed soundtrack plays in the background — bouncy by day, calm at night, floaty in space — with its own **volume slider**. The eagle's cry, the train horn and the crossing bell are real recordings. Settings is split into tabs — **Controls** (camera view, swipe style, D-pad and its layout), **World** (time of day and weather), **Audio** (music and sound effects) and **Display** (graphics quality and title font). Your top score, coins, outfits and missions are saved on your device.
