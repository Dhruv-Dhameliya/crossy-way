# 🐔 Crossy Way

A fast, colourful hopping game. Guide a little chicken across endless roads, rivers and railway tracks — how far can you get?

## How to play

- **Hop forward** to reach new rows. Every new row scores points.
- **Dodge** cars, trucks and buses. Wait for a gap, then go.
- **Cross rivers** by hopping onto floating logs and lily pads. Don't fall in!
- **Watch the tracks.** When the signals flash and you hear the whistle, a train is coming.
- **Don't dawdle.** Reach a new row every few seconds or the eagle swoops in.

You lose if you get hit, fall in the water, or the eagle gets you.

## Scoring

- **Combo** — hop to new rows in quick succession to build a combo. The multiplier climbs from **x1 up to x5**, and every new row is worth the current multiplier. Pause too long and it resets.
- **Close calls** — stand in a lane and let a vehicle sweep past with barely a gap to earn **bonus points** (and keep your combo alive).

## Controls

| Device | Move | Other |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keyboard | Arrow keys or WASD | Space = forward, Esc = pause |
| Touch | Tap = hop forward, swipe = hop that way | Optional on-screen D-pad in Settings |
| Mouse | Click = hop forward, drag = hop that way | |

## Collect

- 🪙 **Coins** — scattered everywhere; your total is saved.
- **Power-ups** — floating badges that appear now and then:

| | Power-up | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🧲 | Magnet | Pulls nearby coins to you |
| 👻 | Ghost | Traffic passes right through you |
| 🛡️ | Shield | Survive one hit |
| ❄️ | Freeze | Stops all traffic and trains for a few seconds |
| 💰 | 2x Coins | Every coin counts double |
| 💣 | Blast | Blows up the vehicles around you |

## Shop 🛍️

Spend your coins on new looks. Pick a **chicken** (Classic, Brownie, Rosy, Sky, Lime, Fire, Midnight, Golden) and dress it up with **outfits** — shades, a mustache, a bow tie, party hat, red cap, cowboy hat, wizard hat or a royal crown. Mix and match: one hat plus one face item at a time.

## Missions 🎯

- **Daily Challenge** — a new goal every day, the same for everyone. Finish it to earn bonus coins and build a 🔥 streak for bigger rewards.
- **Missions** — three rolling goals like "collect 30 coins" or "reach row 50". Complete one for coins and a fresh mission appears.

## The world

The game slowly cycles from **day** to **sunset**, **night** and **dawn** as you run (vehicles switch on their headlights after dark). The **weather** changes too: clear skies, **rain** with thunder and lightning, and **fog**.

## Music & settings

A live-composed soundtrack plays in the background — bouncy by day, calm at night — with its own **volume slider**. In Settings you can also pick the camera view (lean left / right), force day, night or a weather type, choose graphics quality (it auto-adjusts to your device), switch sound effects on or off, change the title font and turn on an on-screen D-pad. Your top score, coins, outfits and missions are saved on your device.

Enjoy the ride! 🚗🚆🐔
