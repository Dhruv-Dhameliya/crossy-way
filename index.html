HOW TO PLAY

THE BASICS
  • 🐔 Hop forward to reach new rows. Quick hops build a combo up to x5.
  • 🚗 Dodge traffic and cross rivers on logs, lily pads and turtles.
  • 😰 Squeeze past a vehicle for a close call bonus.
  • 🪙 Collect coins to buy chickens and outfits in the shop.

WATCH OUT FOR
  • 🚦 Train signals: green = the track is clear, flashing red = a train is about to come.
  • 🐢 Turtles shake, then dive. Hop off before they go under!
  • 🐊 Crocodile logs: the back is safe, but the head snaps shut. Stay away when the mouth opens!
  • 🧊 Ice: you keep sliding a little after you land. Plan your next hop.
  • 🚲 Bike lanes: small, fast bicycles (hover scooters in Space) come in streams. Listen for their bells.
  • 🚆 Double tracks: two railways can fire at once from opposite directions.
  • 🪵 Rotten logs: they shake, then sink. Keep moving!
  • ⏩ Conveyor lanes: the belt carries you sideways while you stand on it. Hop on and keep moving.
  • 🟤 Mud lanes (moon dust in Space): a hop that starts in the mud is slow and low.
  • 🍄 Trampoline lanes (jump pads in Space): land on one and you are flung two rows forward, over anything. Make sure the landing is safe!
  • 🦬 Stampede: a rare lane of running animals. You get a rumble and a warning first.
  • ⚡ Rare events: thunderstorms (solar flares in Space), coin rain, a drone that drops a supply crate on a grass lane, a traffic jam (a road stops and honks: your chance to cross) and fireworks (a short combo boost).
  • 📅 Weekly challenge: one harder goal per week (Monday to Sunday), some tied to a modifier, with a bigger reward and a week streak. It sits under the daily challenge on the Missions screen.
  • 📊 Stats: open Trophies and switch to the Stats tab to see your distance, time played, favourite way to lose and more.
  • 🎲 Modifiers: pick a rule twist (Night Shift, Rush Hour, Penniless, Moon Hops...) on the home screen. Each one has its own top score.
  • 🐥 Shop extras: pets that follow you, trails, auras, new ways to exit and hop sounds. All just for fun.
  • 🧘 Zen mode: no eagle, no timer, lots of coins. Pick it as your difficulty.
  • 🪨 Chasms: only moving platforms cross them. Time your hop, don't fall in.
  • 🚀 Space: run far enough and the world turns into space. Ships fly past steel walkways, rivers are empty space crossed on broken asteroids, comets replace trains (watch the beacons!), laser strikes replace crocodiles and a UFO replaces the eagle. Choose the stage on the home screen.
  • 🦅 The eagle: if you stand still you'll hear it screech. 3 seconds later it grabs you.
  • 🚑 Special vehicles: ambulances, ice-cream trucks, tractors, motorbikes and fast sports cars.
  • 🌲 Hay bales, fences, scarecrows, barrels and more block your way.
